AFIYA COLLECTION

Fabric-first, customer-focused.

Afiya Collection is a ladies clothing and fabric store founded by Atif Ansari. The store primarily specializes in fabric sold by the meter, along with a selection of ready-made ladies clothing and a wide variety of laces and borders.

Customers can browse different fabric categories, check catalogue prices and contact the store directly for availability and product enquiries.
